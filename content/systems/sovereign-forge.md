# Sovereign Psyche Forge

## Overview
The Sovereign Psyche Forge is the internal laboratory where the Sovereign Brain's "truths" are tested, refined, and evolved. It is the mechanism that transforms raw AI output into calibrated, sovereign knowledge.

## Components

### 1. Tension Monitor
The Tension Monitor is the primary sensor of the Forge. It measures the dissonance between a "Smooth" probabilistic output (typical LLM response) and a "Sovereign Anchor" (a verified, immutable truth).

- **Smooth**: Output aligns with anchors; delivery proceeds.
- **Static**: Dissonance detected. Triggers a "Hunt" for missing evidence to resolve the contradiction.
- **Chaos**: High entropy/contradiction detected (Siren's Call). Triggers a Hard Reset to prevent the system from hallucinating a false synthesis.

### 2. Sovereign Revision
Sovereign Revision is the process of evolutionary truth-updating. Rather than simply overwriting a claim, the Forge uses `supersedeClaim` to maintain a lineage.

- **Supersession**: When new evidence emerges, the old claim is marked as `superseded` and linked to the new claim.
- **Lineage**: This maintains a full version history of how a particular truth evolved, providing a transparent audit trail of the brain's epistemic growth.

## The Forge Loop
`Tension Analysis` $\rightarrow$ `Dissonance Detection` $\rightarrow$ `Sovereign Revision` $\rightarrow$ `Anchor Update`
