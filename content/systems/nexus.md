# Sovereign-Nexus Cognitive Ledger

## Overview
The Sovereign-Nexus is the temporal and evolutionary ledger of the Brain's cognitive state. It records not just the current state of a claim, but its *trajectory*.

## Functionality
- **Claim Evolution**: Tracks how a belief moves from `[UNKNOWN]` $\rightarrow$ `[UNCERTAIN]` $\rightarrow$ `[KNOWN]` over time.
- **Revision Tracking**: Records every time a confidence score is updated and the evidence that triggered the revision.
- **Epistemic Continuity**: Ensures that when the Brain revisits a topic, it remembers the *path* taken to reach the current conclusion, preventing circular reasoning.

## Integration
Works closely with the Chronos temporal index and the Mnemosyne Vault.
