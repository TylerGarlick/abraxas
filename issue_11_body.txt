### User Story
As a Compliance Officer, I want a high-throughput parallel review engine (Omniscient Auditor), so that I can audit large documents for epistemic consistency and generate uncertainty heat-maps in minutes.

### Description
The Omniscient Auditor is designed for documents, not real-time queries. It treats a document as a series of atomic propositions and applies the full Sovereign stack to each one, ensuring zero-drift between source and synthesis across large volumes of material.

### Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Implement a parallel document auditing pipeline that decomposes text into atomic propositions.
- [ ] Apply the full Sovereign stack (Janus $\rightarrow$ Soter $\rightarrow$ CVP) to every atomic claim.
- [ ] Generate a visual uncertainty heat-map based on claim-level risk and consensus.
- [ ] Verify that the system can audit a 100-page document in under 5 minutes.

### Implementation Notes
- Uses the "Epistemic Scanner" approach to identify the precise location of hallucinations in static text.
- Integration: Should be callable via `SVR-AUTO-AUDIT` and potentially trigger `Quest-Trigger` for resolved gaps.

### Research Notes
