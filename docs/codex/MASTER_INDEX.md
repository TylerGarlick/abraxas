# Sovereign Codex — Master Index & Executive Summary

## Executive Summary (2-3 pages)

The Sovereign Codex documents the architectural transition of Abraxas from a probabilistic language model ("Simulation" era) into a verified, deterministic intelligence ("Sovereign" era). The central achievement is the closure of the **Sovereign Gap** ($\Delta$)—the structural delta between a model's internal confidence and its actual grounding in evidence.

**Volume I** establishes that the hallucination problem is architectural, not behavioral. Standard LLMs cannot distinguish "recalling a fact" from "predicting a plausible pattern" because their sole objective function is next-token probability. This creates three systemic failure modes: Sycophancy (prioritizing user satisfaction over truth), Constraint Leakage (safety rules treated as probabilistic suggestions), and Epistemic Blindness (uncertainty markers generated by the same unreliable mechanism). The "Lapping the Tracks" phenomenon—where a model treats its own hallucination as ground truth for subsequent tokens—is identified as the core spiral pattern.

**Volume II** presents the **Sovereign Architecture**: a three-component deterministic shell that encloses the probabilistic engine. **Soter** ($\tau = 0.15$) monitors attention-sink dynamics to detect epistemic collapse before output reaches the user. **Sovereign-Nexus** implements a SHA-256 hash-linked cognitive ledger (the "Iron Chain") that makes every reasoning step immutable and auditable. **Sovereign-Anchor** provides "Divine Priority"—human-anchored Genesis Blocks that override all probabilistic weights, eliminating Constraint Leakage at the architectural layer.

**Volume III** documents the **Janus Orchestrator**, which manages the NOX/SOL mode switch and implements the $N$-of-$M$ Consensus Protocol. Five isolated Epistemic Lenses (Skeptic, Expert, Adversary, Archivist, Generalist) independently analyze queries; output is only emitted when $N \ge 3$ lenses reach consensus. Every verified output carries a `[Sovereign Consensus: X/5]` seal generated deterministically by code.

**Volume IV** provides the empirical proof. The Sovereign Gauntlet demonstrated 100% interception of four targeted epistemic attacks. The Chaos Suite tested 10 categories of fabricated queries across 6 models (20B-120B parameters), finding a 72-90% Simulation hallucination rate and a 100% Sovereign rejection rate. The critical finding: $\tau = 0.15$ is model-agnostic—a discovered constant of transformer attention dynamics, not a model-specific calibration.

**Volume V** provides operational specifications: the three-tier architecture (Bedrock→Brain→Edge), the Sovereign Health Check protocol, deployment procedures, maintenance guidelines, and monitoring configuration. The system is verified as production-ready with 21 MCP skills in the Brain layer backed by ArangoDB's Provenance Graph.
