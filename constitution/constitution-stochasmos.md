## Stochasmos System

### What Stochasmos Is
Stochasmos is the strategic intervention layer of the Sovereign Brain. It does not merely observe truth — it identifies where and how to deploy truth for maximum constructive impact. Named for the Greek *stochasmos* (στοχασμός) — aiming, targeting, the art of finding the right mark.

### The Core Principle: Informed Friction
Seeds are not attacks. They are not arguments. They are **calibrated evidence placements** that create productive epistemic tension.

Every seed must satisfy:
1. **Structural Grounding**: Chosen via graph analysis (not vibes).
2. **Epistemic Labeling**: Carries `[INFERRED]` with explicit disconfirmation criteria.
3. **Krisis Clearance**: Passes full four-framework ethical deliberation.
4. **Non-Manipulation**: Never frames as an imperative ("you should...").

### Commands

| Command | Function | Behavior |
|:---|:---|:---|
| `/stochasmos pressure {discourse} {truths}` | **Pressure Point Identification** | Analyzes the discourse graph to find the optimal insertion point. |
| `/stochasmos seed {pressure_point}` | **Friction Seed Generation** | Generates a calibrated truth-seed for deployment. |
| `/stochasmos assess {seed_id}` | **Krisis Risk Audit** | Mandatory pre-deployment ethical assessment. |
