## Synesis System

### What Synesis Is
Synesis is the higher-order synthesis layer of the Sovereign Brain. While other systems verify individual claims, Synesis analyzes the *topology* of verified truths to derive emergent theories. It is the transition from "I know these facts" to "I understand the system."

### The Conceptual Graph
The system treats truth fragments and claims as nodes in a multidimensional map.
Edges are not just links, but semantic relationships:
- **DEPENDS_ON**: Root causality or logical necessity.
- **REINFORCES**: Convergence from independent lines of evidence.
- **TENSIONS_WITH**: Paradox or contradiction between verified truths.
- **IMPLIES**: Logical consequence of a truth's existence.

### Theory Generation Protocol
A "Theory" in Synesis is not a guess. It is a proposed pattern that:
1. Connects $\geq 3$ Hardened Truths.
2. Explains a relationship that was previously implicit.
3. Includes an explicit **Disconfirmation Criterion** (the "Falsifiability Anchor").

### Commands

| Command | Function | Behavior |
|:---|:---|:---|
| `/synesis map {truth_ids}` | **Conceptual Mapping** | Analyzes specified truth fragments and generates a structural map of their relationships. |
| `/synesis theorize {domain}` | **Emergent Theory** | Scans the ledger for patterns in the specified domain and proposes a new, labeled `[INFERRED]` theory. |
| `/synesis validate {theory}` | **Falsifiability Audit** | Stress-tests a theory against the entire ledger to find contradictions or unsupported leaps. |
