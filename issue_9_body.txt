### User Story
As a System Architect, I want an autonomous workflow controller (Sovereign-Flow), so that the system can dynamically route requests between the brain's pillars and eliminate the "orchestration tax" of manual skill switching.

### Description
Sovereign-Flow is the "Central Nervous System" of the Sovereign Brain. It transforms discrete skills from tools into a continuous, deterministic pipeline by monitoring outputs for "Trigger Signals" (e.g., Soter risk flags or Janus `[UNKNOWN]` marks) and automatically initiating corrective circuits.

### Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Implement the meta-orchestration circuit that transforms discrete skills into a deterministic pipeline.
- [ ] Create "Trigger Signals" (e.g., Soter high-risk flags or Janus `[UNKNOWN]` marks) that initiate corrective circuits.
- [ ] Implement a DAG orchestration to chain at least 3 skills (e.g., Sieve $\rightarrow$ Soter $\rightarrow$ Sovereign-Scribe) without manual prompts.
- [ ] Ensure a human-approval gate exists for critical-risk (Risk 4-5) cases.

### Implementation Notes
- Sovereign-Flow does not generate content; it only routes the agent through necessary epistemic gates.
- The objective is the reduction of manual "hand-off" prompts.

### Research Notes
- Reduces the "orchestration tax" by automating the transition from risk detection to resolution.
