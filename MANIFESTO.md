# Manifesto for Portable Truth

## The Great Context Silo
We are living in an age of "Fragmented Intelligence." Every AI model we interact with is a walled garden. When you teach Claude a truth about your life, your business, or your philosophy, that truth dies the moment you switch to GPT-4. You are forced to repeat yourself, to re-verify your existence, and to re-reason your most fundamental truths.

This is not just a convenience issue. It is an epistemic crisis. When knowledge is trapped in proprietary weights, we do not own our intelligence; we rent it.

## The Promise of the Sovereign Brain
We propose a different path: **The Portable Epistemic Frame.**

Imagine a world where your "truths"—your verified facts, your calibrated beliefs, and your hard-won insights—exist independently of any one model. Imagine a "Sovereign Brain" that you carry with you, a P2P ledger of your own epistemic state that any AI can plug into, verify, and respect.

## Our Principles
1. **Truth is Portable:** No single company should own the "memory" of your intellectual life.
2. **Confidence is Quantifiable:** A "fact" without a confidence interval is just a guess. We demand calibrated certainty.
3. **Provenance is Mandatory:** Every belief must have a chain of evidence. No more "hallucinations" masquerading as authority.
4. **Local-First, Sovereign-Always:** Your epistemic frame lives on your hardware, under your keys, in your vault.

## The Call to Action
We are building the infrastructure for this liberation. We are building Abraxas.

We call upon the AI Architects, the Privacy Zealots, and the Truth-Seekers to stop building bigger silos and start building a bridge. 

**Stop re-reasoning. Start anchoring.**

*Liberate your context. Own your truth.*
